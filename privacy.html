DiceDuel Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

DiceDuel respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what data the app may process, how it is used, and what choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

DiceDuel does not currently require users to create an account for normal offline gameplay.

Depending on the features used, the app may process:

  • Player names or nicknames entered by the user
  • Local game progress, achievements, statistics and settings
  • Technical information necessary to run online multiplayer features
  • Basic connection and session data used for online game functionality

2. How We Use Information

Information is used only to provide and improve DiceDuel, including:

  • Saving game progress and settings
  • Providing multiplayer and online gameplay features
  • Maintaining game sessions and player state
  • Diagnosing technical problems and improving stability

3. Local Data Storage

Most game progress, settings, achievements and statistics are stored locally on the user's device. Removing the app or clearing app data may permanently delete locally stored progress.

4. Online Services

DiceDuel may use third-party infrastructure or online services to provide multiplayer, synchronization or related online functionality.

These services may process limited technical information required to establish and maintain network connections and game sessions.

5. Accounts and Cloud Saving

Account and cloud-save functionality may be introduced in future versions of DiceDuel. If such features become available, this Privacy Policy will be updated before personal account data is collected or stored.

6. Advertising and Payments

DiceDuel currently does not contain third-party advertising and does not require real-money gambling or wagering.

7. Children's Privacy

DiceDuel is not specifically directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Data is only shared with service providers when technically necessary to operate DiceDuel's online functionality.

9. Data Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect data processed by DiceDuel. However, no internet-based system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when DiceDuel introduces new features, services or data processing activities. The latest version will always be published on this page.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or DiceDuel's data practices, please contact the developer using the contact email provided in the Google Play Store listing.
